# Venue data checklist

For capturing the building at the event: the James West Alumni Center, unless that changes. Capture as early as possible.

## Scan (Polycam on an iPhone with LiDAR)

- Cover the floors and rooms the demo will use, plus every exit, stairwell and corridor between them.
- Walk slowly and get each exit from inside and outside.
- Export GLB; Aria's viewer loads GLB and USDZ. Check that your Polycam plan allows the export.
- Upload the mesh to the new Supabase project's storage, not to git.

## Floor plan

- Photograph the posted evacuation maps, usually near elevators and stairwells. They show exits and routes.
- Ask the organizers or venue staff whether a floor plan is available.
- Online: a quick search found no floor plan. The Alumni Center's rental page (alumni.ucla.edu/alumni-center/renting) lists room capacities and links a short walkthrough video for each room. The venue's reservations team (reservations@alumni.ucla.edu, 310-825-2586) may have one.
- Measure one known distance, like a doorway or a corridor, so the plan can be scaled.

## Building details

- Address: 325 Westwood Plaza, Los Angeles, CA 90095. For coordinates, right-click the building in Google Maps; the map and the seed data need them.
- Number of floors; year built and construction type, if posted or findable
- Occupancy limits (often posted per room). From the rental page, standing capacity: Collins Conference Room 250, Collins Patio 320, Founders' Room 150, Founders' Patio 200, Galleria 270
- Exits: how many, where they lead, which are stairs and which are at ground level, and the widths of two or three doors
- Fire equipment: extinguishers, alarm pulls, sprinklers, and hydrants or standpipes outside
- Photos of the exits, the stairwells and each side of the building's exterior

## Permission

- Check with the organizers before scanning areas beyond the hacking space.
